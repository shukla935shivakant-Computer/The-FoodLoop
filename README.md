## 🎯 Problem Statement
Globally, over **1.3 billion tons of food is wasted annually**, resulting in **$1.2 trillion in economic losses** and 8–10% of global greenhouse emissions[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Meanwhile, millions suffer from food insecurity due to fragmented, slow, and unorganized food rescue supply chains.

## 💡 The Solution
**FoodLoop Global** is an intelligent redistribution platform built by **Team Worthen Fires** that bridges the gap between surplus food vendors and verified local receivers in real time:

* 🏪 **Dynamic Surplus Listing:** Restaurants, events, and vendors quickly list surplus food with expiration tracking.
* 🤖 **AI-Powered Matching:** Intelligent geolocation algorithms match surplus donors with nearby NGOs and food banks in under 5 seconds.
* 📦 **Verified Hand-off:** ID-verified transfer process and QR code tracking ensure food safety and complete supply chain transparency.

---

## 🔥 Key Features
- 🧠 **Smart Matching Engine:** Considers food urgency, travel distance, and receiver capacity to prevent bottlenecks.
- 📈 **Proactive Supply Prediction:** Uses historical data to predict seasonal donation patterns before surplus occurs.
- 🔗 **Trust-Verifiable Supply Chain:** Immutable tracking log for complete transparency between donors, receivers, and admins.
- 📊 **Real-time Impact Analytics:** Live dashboard tracking meals delivered, waste diverted, and carbon offset generated.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Redux Toolkit
- **Backend:** Node.js (API Gateway), Python (Data Processing)
- **AI / ML Core:** TensorFlow (Predictive Routing & Expiry Analytics)
- **Database & Cache:** PostgreSQL, Redis, Firebase
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes, AWS (EC2/S3), Prometheus & Grafana
